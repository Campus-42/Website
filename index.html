FAQs

Who is this app for?

This app is primarily for universities who wish to centralise their communication with students into one place.

How can my university use this?

By simply contacting us at hello@campus42.co.uk

What available features are there?

Currently, students are able to create and host events & societies, which may be location based. Chat functionality has been included and allows for communication amongst peers.
